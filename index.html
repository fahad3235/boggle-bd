        How to play

        1. Tap adjacent tiles (diagonals count) to build a word.
        2. Words must be at least 3 letters long.
        3. A Q tile counts as “Qu”.
        4. Longer words = more points: 3–4 letters = 1, 5 = 2, 6 = 3, 7 = 5, 8+ = 11.
        5. When time runs out, see the words you missed.

        Offline word game. Not affiliated with Boggle or Hasbro. Dictionary: the 20,000 most-common English words (first20hours/google-10000-english), filtered to 3–8 letters. Board letters are drawn from English letter frequencies, not the licensed Boggle dice set. All data stays in your browser (localStorage).
